Business Showcase

This page presents selected projects that demonstrate my ability to apply business knowledge, technical skills and problem-solving approaches to real-world situations. The projects below highlight my experience in business operations, data analysis and digital transformation.

Project 1: Wholesale Business Operations Improvement

Project Overview

This project involved supporting the daily operations of a wholesale catering supplies business. The focus was improving operational efficiency, maintaining customer satisfaction and ensuring product availability.

My Role

My role involved supporting warehouse operations, monitoring stock availability, communicating with suppliers and assisting customers with product enquiries.

Business Problem

The business experienced situations where customers requested items that were temporarily unavailable. This created challenges such as delayed orders, customer dissatisfaction and potential lost sales.

Solution

I helped identify alternative suppliers and replacement products where possible. By communicating with suppliers and managing availability information, suitable alternatives could be provided to customers more efficiently.

Outcome

The approach helped maintain customer relationships, reduce disruption to orders and improve the overall handling of stock availability issues.

Skills & Approaches Used

  • Problem Solving
  • Supplier Communication
  • Customer Service
  • Business Operations
  • Decision Making

Project 3: Bella Vista Restaurant Digital Transformation Website

Project Overview

This project involved designing and developing a restaurant website that demonstrates how digital technology can improve customer experience and support business growth.

My Role

I acted as the website developer and technology consultant. My responsibility was to plan the website structure, identify business needs and create digital features that improve communication between the restaurant and its customers.

Business Problem

The restaurant had limited digital presence and relied heavily on traditional communication methods. Customers had no simple way to submit booking requests, provide feedback or access important restaurant information online.

Solution

I developed a restaurant website that includes:

  • Restaurant information and services
  • Contact details
  • Social media integration
  • Online booking request form
  • Customer feedback submission
  • Automatic email generation using JavaScript
  • Privacy and GDPR considerations

Development Rationale

The structure of the Problem-Solving Page was designed around the customer journey. The website begins by introducing the restaurant, then provides customers with essential information before allowing them to interact through booking and feedback features.

The booking form was created because restaurants depend heavily on customer reservations. Instead of developing a complex database system, JavaScript was used to generate an email containing the customer's information, providing a simple and effective solution.

Social media links were included to improve customer engagement, while privacy information and cookie reminders demonstrate awareness of responsible data handling and GDPR requirements.

Outcome

The completed website provides Bella Vista Restaurant with a stronger digital presence, improved customer communication and a practical solution for managing online enquiries.

Skills & Approaches Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • Responsive Web Development
  • User Experience Design
  • Digital Transformation
  • Business Analysis
