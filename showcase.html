Project 1: Order Availability Workflow Improvement

Project Overview

An operations improvement initiative based on recurring stock availability issues at a wholesale catering supplies business. The goal was to give staff a consistent way to find alternatives before an order was delayed or lost.

My Role

I handled stock and product enquiries, checked possible substitutes, contacted suppliers about availability and shared options with customers. I focused on making the response repeatable, rather than treating each unavailable item as an isolated problem.

Business Problem

The business experienced situations where customers requested items that were temporarily unavailable. This created challenges such as delayed orders, customer dissatisfaction and potential lost sales.

Solution

I helped identify alternative suppliers and replacement products where possible. By communicating with suppliers and managing availability information, suitable alternatives could be provided to customers more efficiently.

Outcome

The result was a practical alternative-sourcing workflow for customer enquiries. Its measurable success metric is the percentage of availability-related enquiries resolved with an alternative rather than a cancelled or delayed order. Historical figures were not recorded, so I am not claiming an unsupported percentage improvement.

Project 3: Bella Vista Restaurant Digital Transformation Website

Project Overview

This project involved designing and developing a restaurant website that demonstrates how digital technology can improve customer experience and support business growth.

My Role

I acted as the website developer and technology consultant. My responsibility was to plan the website structure, identify business needs and create digital features that improve communication between the restaurant and its customers.

Business Problem

The restaurant had limited digital presence and relied heavily on traditional communication methods. Customers had no simple way to submit booking requests, provide feedback or access important restaurant information online.

Solution

I developed a restaurant website that includes:

  • Restaurant information and services
  • Contact details
  • Social media integration
  • Online booking request form
  • Customer feedback submission
  • Automatic email generation using JavaScript
  • Privacy notice and consent experience

Development Rationale

I built the page around the customer journey: menu and offer first, then practical details, then booking or feedback. This makes the important actions easier to find than placing them among long blocks of business analysis.

The booking form was created because restaurants depend heavily on customer reservations. I chose a mailto hand-off instead of a database because this is a small demonstration with no server or secure storage. JavaScript validates the booking details and prepares a message for staff; the page makes clear that the customer still has to send that email.

I used semantic headings, explicit form labels, required-field validation, keyboard-visible focus styles and a reduced-motion stylesheet to support accessibility. The privacy note explains what the demo stores locally and what happens to form data.

Outcome

The completed website provides Bella Vista Restaurant with a stronger digital presence, improved customer communication and a practical solution for managing online enquiries.
