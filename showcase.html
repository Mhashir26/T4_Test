Business Showcase

This page highlights projects that demonstrate my technical, analytical and business problem-solving skills. Each project reflects my ability to identify challenges, develop effective solutions and work professionally within different business environments.

Project 1 - Wholesale Inventory Management

My Role

I supported the daily operations of a wholesale catering supplies business by managing stock levels, monitoring inventory and communicating with suppliers.

Problem

Customers frequently requested products that were temporarily out of stock. Without an organised process, this could lead to delays, cancelled orders and reduced customer satisfaction.

Solution

I contacted alternative suppliers, checked stock availability and arranged replacement deliveries wherever possible. This ensured customers received suitable alternatives within a short time frame.

Outcome

The business maintained strong customer relationships, reduced lost sales and improved overall operational efficiency.

Skills Used

  • Problem Solving
  • Communication
  • Inventory Management
  • Supplier Negotiation
  • Business Operations

Project 3 - Restaurant Digital Transformation Website

My Role

I designed and developed a responsive portfolio website that includes a restaurant digital transformation solution as part of my Web Application Development module.

Problem

Many local restaurants rely on traditional methods of customer communication, making it difficult to manage bookings, promote services and engage customers online.

Solution

I developed a modern website featuring business information, contact details, social media integration and an interactive booking and enquiry form. The design focuses on accessibility, responsiveness and ease of use across different devices.

Development Rationale

I structured the Problem-Solving Page to provide users with quick access to the restaurant's essential information while encouraging interaction through a simple booking form. The layout follows a logical flow from business introduction to customer engagement features, making the website intuitive and easy to navigate. Responsive design principles and clear navigation were prioritised to improve user experience on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Outcome

The completed solution demonstrates how web technologies can improve customer engagement, support digital marketing and streamline communication between customers and the restaurant.

Skills Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • Responsive Web Design
  • User Experience (UX)
  • Digital Transformation
