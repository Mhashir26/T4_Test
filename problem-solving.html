Restaurant Digital Transformation: Problem-Solving Case Study

This page demonstrates my ability to analyse a real business challenge and develop a technology-based solution. The case study focuses on Bella Vista Italian Restaurant, a fictional restaurant that requires digital improvements to enhance customer experience, improve communication and increase operational efficiency.

Business Context

Bella Vista Restaurant provides dine-in services but currently relies mainly on traditional methods such as telephone bookings and face-to-face communication. Although the restaurant provides quality food and service, limited digital systems create challenges when managing customer enquiries, promoting services and collecting feedback.

The objective of this digital transformation project is to create a website solution that improves customer interaction while reducing pressure on restaurant staff.

Identified Business Problems

After analysing the restaurant's current processes, the following business problems were identified:

  • Problem 1: Limited booking system
    Customers must contact the restaurant by telephone, which can lead to missed calls and lost reservations during busy periods.

  • Problem 2: Poor online presence
    Customers cannot easily access restaurant information such as opening hours, location, menu highlights and services.

  • Problem 3: Lack of customer feedback system
    The restaurant has no structured digital method for collecting customer opinions and improving service quality.

  • Problem 4: Limited customer engagement
    Without social media integration, the restaurant has fewer opportunities to communicate promotions and connect with customers.

Proposed Digital Solution

To solve these challenges, I developed a responsive restaurant website that acts as a central digital platform for customers. The solution focuses on improving communication, accessibility and customer engagement.

Implemented Features

  • Online booking request form
  • Customer feedback submission
  • Restaurant contact information
  • Opening hours and services
  • Social media integration
  • Automatic email generation using JavaScript
  • Privacy and GDPR considerations

How The Solution Addresses The Problems

Privacy, Security and GDPR Considerations

Customer information collected through the form should only be used for responding to booking requests and enquiries. The website does not permanently store customer information. Users are informed about how their data is used, supporting GDPR principles such as transparency and responsible data handling.

A cookie consent reminder should also be displayed to inform users that cookies may be used to improve website functionality and user experience.
