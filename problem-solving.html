Restaurant Digital Transformation Proposal

Prepared by Muhammad Hashir

Role: Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

As the Chief Technology Officer, I have analysed Bella Vista Italian Restaurant's current business operations and identified several areas where technology can improve customer experience, operational efficiency and business growth. The aim of this project is to create a modern digital platform that increases customer engagement, simplifies restaurant bookings and strengthens the restaurant's online presence.

Current Business Challenges

After analysing the business, I identified several challenges that are limiting the restaurant's growth.

  • ❌ Customers can only book tables by telephone.
  • ❌ Busy staff often miss customer calls during peak hours.
  • ❌ Customers cannot easily view menus before visiting.
  • ❌ The restaurant has limited online visibility.
  • ❌ No digital system exists for collecting customer feedback.
  • ❌ Promotions are difficult to advertise quickly.
  • ❌ No loyalty programme encourages repeat customers.

Digital Transformation Solution

To solve these business challenges, I designed a modern web application that provides customers with quick access to restaurant services while reducing staff workload.

Key Features

  • ✔ Online booking request form
  • ✔ Digital restaurant information
  • ✔ Customer feedback submission
  • ✔ Social media integration
  • ✔ Contact details available 24/7
  • ✔ Mobile responsive website
  • ✔ Automatic email generation
  • ✔ GDPR compliant privacy information

How Each Feature Solves a Business Problem

Business Problem Digital Solution
Missed telephone bookings Online booking request form available 24/7
No customer feedback Interactive feedback form
Poor online engagement Social media integration
Customers cannot find restaurant information Dedicated contact page with address and opening hours
Low customer retention Promotions and loyalty scheme announcements
Slow communication Automatic booking email generation

Expected Business Benefits

  • 📈 Increase table bookings
  • 📈 Improve customer satisfaction
  • 📈 Reduce staff workload
  • 📈 Increase repeat customers
  • 📈 Improve online visibility
  • 📈 Faster customer communication
  • 📈 Strengthen Bella Vista's digital presence
